Who We Are

A private selection, built one piece at a time.

Philosophy

Emilov Luxury began as a selection shared with a small audience on Instagram. The website exists to bring the same care to a wider circle of collectors, with more context on every piece.

Curation

Every piece is chosen personally, not algorithmically. We would rather offer twenty pieces we believe in than two hundred we don't — the collection stays intentionally small.

How We Work

By appointment, with clients followed remotely and by shipment where needed. We answer questions before you buy, not just after — trust matters more to us than any single sale.
