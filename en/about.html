Who We Are

A private showroom, built one piece at a time.

Philosophy

Emilov Luxury began as a curated selection shared with a small, attentive audience on Instagram. The website exists to bring the same care — and a great deal more transparency — to a wider circle of collectors. CONFIRM: founder's own account of how and when Emilov started

Expertise

Every piece is chosen personally, not algorithmically. We look for condition, rarity and lasting relevance — not simply what will sell fastest. CONFIRM: relevant background/experience to state publicly

Curation

We would rather offer twenty pieces we believe in than two hundred we don't. The collection stays intentionally small.

Location

By appointment in Milan, with a show room in Rome — serving clients internationally by appointment and by shipment. CONFIRM: exact addresses

Our Approach to Clients

We answer questions before you buy, not just after. If a piece isn't right for you, we'll say so. Long-term trust matters more to us than any single sale.
