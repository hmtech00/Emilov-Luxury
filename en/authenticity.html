The Principle

Trust is built from the details.

Buying a piece you haven't held is an act of trust. Here's what matters to us before a piece reaches the collection.

Condition

Every piece is described with its real condition — Excellent, Very Good, or Good — with notes on corners, hardware, lining and any wear.

Excellent

Little to no visible signs of wear; hardware and structure like new.

Very Good

Gently used with minor, honestly noted signs of wear.

Good

Visible wear consistent with age and use — always disclosed clearly in the listing.

Product Detail

We photograph the actual item for sale — not stock imagery — and show hardware, interior and any imperfections, so you know exactly what you're looking at.

Documentation

Where original receipts, boxes or paperwork exist, they are noted in the listing and included with the piece.

Direct Communication

If something isn't right about a piece, we won't offer it. And if you have a question about a specific piece, we'd rather answer it before you buy, not after.
