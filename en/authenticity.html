Authenticity

Buying a piece you haven't held is an act of trust. Here is exactly how we try to earn it — and where we're still building the process further.

Our Standard

Every piece offered by Emilov Luxury is reviewed before it is listed. We look at construction, hardware, materials, stitching and proportions against what is known for the brand, model and production period in question. If we have any doubt about a piece, it does not go on the site. CONFIRM: describe actual current process in the founder's own words

Authentication Process

Our review currently combines hands-on inspection with reference material for hardware stamps, date codes, materials and construction details specific to each maison. CONFIRM: is a third-party authentication service used for higher-value pieces? Name it only if true.

Condition Assessment

Each piece is graded and described honestly — Excellent, Very Good, or Good — with specific notes on corners, hardware, lining and any wear, illustrated with photographs of the actual item.

Excellent

Little to no visible signs of wear; hardware and structure like new.

Very Good

Gently used with minor, honestly noted signs of wear.

Good

Visible wear consistent with age and use — always disclosed clearly in the listing.

Collector's Piece

Rare or vintage items assessed on their own merits, condition notes included.

Documentation & Provenance

Where original receipts, boxes or paperwork exist, they are noted in the listing and included with the piece. Where they don't, we say so plainly rather than imply otherwise. CONFIRM

Transparency

We photograph the actual item for sale — not stock imagery — and disclose imperfections rather than hide them. If something isn't right about a piece, we won't offer it.

Questions

Have a question about a specific piece, or about our process in general? We're glad to talk it through before you buy.
